## Final prompt

Use case: illustration-story.
Asset type: a small static hero illustration for a personal machine learning portfolio with a pastel journal/craft aesthetic.
Primary request: a friendly cartoon person working at a computer.
Subject and scene: one adult person with dark hair, seated at a simple desk, hands naturally on a keyboard, concentrating on a desktop monitor; include a small smiling coffee mug on the desk.
Style: charming flat hand-drawn sticker illustration, rounded simplified forms, clean slightly imperfect soft dark outlines, minimal detail, no realism, no gradients or heavy shadows. Warm cream, pale blush pink, soft sage/mint, and a small amount of lavender. The illustration should feel at home next to playful scrapbook icons.
Composition: compact balanced scene, three-quarter side view so the person and computer are both recognizable, isolated with generous transparent margins, suitable for the right side of a website hero and readable at 300 pixels wide.
Constraints: genuinely transparent background; no room or scenery; no text, lettering, symbols, watermarks, or brand logos. This is a generic illustration, not a portrait of the site owner.
