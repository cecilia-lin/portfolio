The question

How can we turn a subjective assessment of content credibility into a structured, testable process? And when comparing agent designs, does additional reasoning complexity help?

Context & data

Credibility involves several different judgments. The project separates them into six signals: news coverage, intent, stance, sensationalism, context veracity, and title–body alignment.

Predictive components use labeled datasets, including LIAR-PLUS for stance. Article-level labeled data supports evaluation of the reasoning agents. The repository includes training code, experiment notebooks, and saved per-factor results.

My contribution

I led a three-person team building the combined ML and LLM pipeline. My work included structuring the six-dimension evaluation rubric, comparing competing designs through controlled validation, and documenting the evaluation logic for reuse.

Approach

  1. Decompose the task. Evaluate distinct credibility signals instead of treating credibility as a single opaque label.
  2. Combine models and agents. Use predictive signals alongside LLM-based article analysis.
  3. Compare designs. Evaluate simpler agents against structured and fractal reasoning variants using labeled examples and per-factor accuracy.
  4. Document the choice. Select the evidence-backed approach and retain the evaluation process.

Evaluation & outcome

Validation result

The more complex approach underperformed the simpler one in our validation. We selected the simpler design based on the evidence.

The result is a modular content-review pipeline and a documented way to compare designs. Per-factor results are important: performance on one signal should not be presented as accuracy for the entire system.

Limitations

Credibility labels depend on the rubric and labeled examples. These experiments do not establish universal fact-checking reliability, and results may vary across topics, datasets, and agent configurations.

The headline lesson is about evaluation and design choice. Refer to the repository’s saved results for the individual factor measurements and configurations.
